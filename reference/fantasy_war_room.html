    How the numbers are made

    Built from your league's Sleeper data: rosters, schedule, weekly stats, injury tags, depth charts, and Sleeper's weekly projections through week 17.

    Where the data comes from

    Everything on this page is pulled straight from Sleeper's public API by your own browser. No account, no server, nothing stored anywhere but your device.

    • Refresh from Sleeper. Pulls your league again: rosters, matchups, weekly stats, injuries and Sleeper's projections through the playoffs. Takes a few seconds.
    • Any league. Type any Sleeper username. If that manager is in several leagues, pick one from the League menu. A link ending in #username opens straight to that manager.
    • Your league's rules. Scoring per catch, passing touchdowns and tight-end bonuses, roster slots, playoff spots, divisions and FAAB budget all come from the league's own settings.
    • Last pull. —, week —.

    Projection check

    How far off Sleeper's projection has been lately, scored with your league's settings.

    In offline testing on 2026 data, gradient boosting on form, opportunity, snap share and matchup, a residual correction, and a season-average blend all missed by more than Sleeper's projection on held-out weeks. So player means use the projection, and the modeling goes where it holds up: floors and ceilings calibrated on the last few weeks, matchup grades from points each defense has allowed, and the season simulation.

    What feeds each view

    • Floors and ceilings. 20th and 80th percentile of actual-to-projected points by position over the last few weeks. On the latest week, held out, they caught — of outcomes against a 60% target.
    • Season simulation. Each team's weekly score is its best lineup's expected points plus noise of — points, the spread of real results around projections. The page runs it in your browser, so what-ifs re-simulate instantly.
    • Clinch marks. Strict math, never simulation. x means no combination of remaining results can knock the team out, using real results plus any playoff-machine picks.
    • Assumptions. Other managers set their best lineup from next week on. Questionable players count at 85%, doubtful at 30%, out at zero for the current week. IR players count again four weeks out unless you set a return week. Trades take effect next week.
